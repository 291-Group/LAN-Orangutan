Appearance

Scanning

Also check each device on a short list of well-known ports (web, printer, NAS, and so on) to identify it more precisely and flag a web interface. Sends a little more traffic and takes a little longer, so it is off by default. Applies on the next scan.

How often the background scanner re-scans while continuous scan is on. Takes effect on the next cycle. Default: 300 seconds.

Networks

Ignore the auto-detected interfaces and scan only the "Networks to scan" list below. Useful on a host with many Docker bridges, VLANs, or routed interfaces.

A routed network (reached through a router, not on a local interface) is scanned but has no MAC addresses. Applies on the next scan. Saved to this instance, not your config file.

Device Groups

Rename a group to fit your own kit, or add ones that suit it better. Renaming updates every device already in that group. Deleting a group leaves its devices ungrouped, it never deletes a device.

Changes apply immediately. Saved to this instance, not your config file.

Storage

A device not seen for this many days is removed. Devices you have labelled, grouped, or added notes to, and devices currently online, are always kept. 0 keeps them forever. Default: 90.

When the list grows past this, the devices seen longest ago are dropped first (labelled, grouped, noted, and online devices are kept). 0 means no cap. Default: 1000.

Changes apply immediately: lowering these can remove stale devices right away. Saved to this instance, not your config file.
